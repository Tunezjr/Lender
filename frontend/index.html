FAQ

What is the maximum LTV?

30% of the NFT’s oracle value. You cannot borrow more than that.

How long is a loan?

Fixed 7-day terms in the product UI. Repay early anytime to unlock the NFT.

Is it custodial?

No. Collateral sits in protocol contracts. Only you can repay and unlock.
