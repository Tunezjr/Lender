FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which wallets are supported?

MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Rabby, and any standard browser wallet that supports Monad. This UI also offers a local preview wallet.

What is the maximum LTV?

30% of the NFT’s value. You cannot borrow more than that.

How long is a loan?

Fixed 7-day terms. Repay early anytime to unlock the NFT.

Are there any fees?

A 3% fee is shown on the loan terms screen before you confirm.

What happens if I don't repay?

Repaid on time → NFT returns to you. Unpaid past due date → NFT releases to the lender per escrow rules.

Is my NFT ever at risk from a hack or admin action?

Collateral is held in non-custodial contracts. There is no company wallet holding your NFT.
